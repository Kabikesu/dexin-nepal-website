Coffee Products

Cocoa Drink Premix with Ganoderma Extract (Cocozhi)

Cocozhi is formulated from the finest cocoa with Ganoderma extract. Pour the Cocozhi powder into a cup, add hot water or milk and stir. It can also be enjoyed as a cold drink.

Coffee Drink Premix with Ganoderma Extract (Lingzhi Coffee 3 in 1)

DXN Lingzhi Coffee 3 in 1 is a mixture of coffee powder and Ganoderma mushroom. Pour the powder into a cup, add hot water and stir well to enjoy the original taste.

Coffee Drink Premix with Cordyceps Powder (Cordyceps Coffee 3 in 1)

DXN Cordyceps Coffee 3 in 1 is a mixture of coffee powder and Cordyceps. Pour the powder into a cup, add hot water and stir well to enjoy the original taste.
