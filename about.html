About Us

Dexin Manufacturing Nepal Pvt. Ltd. is a manufacturing facility in Nepal connected with the wider DXN heritage.

WHO WE ARE

A manufacturing facility built around quality

Dexin Manufacturing Nepal Pvt. Ltd. represents a local manufacturing presence in Nepal, with operations focused on disciplined production, quality and continuous improvement.

Our facility and people support the delivery of products while maintaining a strong focus on manufacturing standards, hygiene and operational responsibility.

DXN GLOBAL HERITAGE

Part of a wider DXN journey

DXN's heritage is closely associated with its founder, Datuk Dr Lim Siow Jin, and the development of products and business activities built around Ganoderma and wellness.

Dexin Manufacturing Nepal carries this broader DXN heritage into a Nepal-based manufacturing environment while maintaining its own local operational focus.
