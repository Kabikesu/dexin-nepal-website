DEXIN GLOBAL HERITAGE

A Legacy Founded on Health & Innovation

DEXIN was established in Malaysia in 1993 by Datuk Lim Siow Jin, whose interest in the health benefits of mushrooms led to a strong focus on Ganoderma or Lingzhi.

Founded in 1993

DEXIN was founded by Datuk Lim Siow Jin, a graduate of the Indian Institute of Technology. His interest in mushrooms and their potential benefits for human health became the foundation for the development of DEXIN.

Since its establishment, DEXIN has developed activities spanning cultivation, manufacturing and marketing, while expanding its product portfolio and international operations.

Today, DEXIN operates across international markets and has built its identity around quality, health-focused products and an integrated business approach.

1993

The Beginning of DEXIN

DEXIN was established in Malaysia with a vision centered around health, quality and integrated manufacturing.

OUR FOUNDER

Datuk Lim Siow Jin

Founder of DEXIN

Datuk Lim Siow Jin founded DEXIN after developing a deep interest in the potential benefits of mushrooms for human health.

His research and interest in Ganoderma, also known as Lingzhi, became an important foundation for DEXIN's development and its focus on health-related products.

His vision helped shape DEXIN's integrated approach to cultivation, manufacturing and marketing, creating the foundation for the company's international growth.
